Install the buttons

On an M5Stack Basic (16 MB), straight from your browser.

1
Open this page on a computerIn Chrome or Edge, not on a phone.
2
Connect the M5StackWith a USB-C data cable.
3
Click InstallChoose the M5Stack's port and confirm.

This browser cannot install: use Chrome or Edge on a computer. Open this page over https.

This is for the first install: it erases the M5Stack, including a saved PIN and wifi. After that the buttons update themselves over wifi.

Then on the M5Stack

⚙︎
Hold the right button 1.5 sThe settings: enter the robot's PIN, choose the robot and set up wifi.
A
Left, middle and rightStart route A, B and C. Hold left for the schedule, hold middle for manual driving.

Unofficial software, use at your own risk and keep the robot in sight. More in the README.
