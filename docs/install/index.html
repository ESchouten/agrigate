Install the buttons

Scraper Control on an M5Stack Basic (16 MB), straight from your browser.

  1. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer (not a phone).
  2. Connect the M5Stack with a USB-C data cable.
  3. Click Install, choose the M5Stack's port and confirm.

This browser cannot install: use Chrome or Edge on a computer. Open this page over https.

This is for the first install: it erases the M5Stack, including a saved PIN and wifi. After that the buttons update themselves over wifi.

Then on the M5Stack
  1. Hold the right button 1.5 s for the settings: enter the robot's PIN, choose the robot and set up wifi.
  2. Left, middle and right start route A, B and C. Hold left for the schedule, hold middle for manual driving.

Unofficial software, use at your own risk and keep the robot in sight. More in the README.
